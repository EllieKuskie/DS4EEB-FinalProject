# DS4EEB-FinalProject

## Ellie Kuskie

## Motivating Question

How do different pollinator orders differ in their visitation frequency to flowers of different shapes.

## Rational For The Question

Pollinators tend to visit particular flowers based on a variety of factors, one of these being flower shape. If morphologies are not compatible then insects will not visit the flower and therefore not pollinate. It is then important to look at which shape of flowers different groups of pollinators are visiting more frequently

## Background Information

Gardens are an important refuge for insect pollinators in the face of increased urbanization. More information about what preferences different groups of pollinators have can help to best maximize the variety of pollinators. This can help to maintain biodiversity at a time where so many insects are being lost to climate change and urbanization. Pollinators visit flowers based upon a variety of factors such as flower color, nectar reward, and flower shape. I want to examine one of these factors, shape, as I believe it is incredibly important for pollinators. Some nectar and/or pollen in flowers may only be accessible to pollinators with particular traits. Some of these traits are more general across orders so I will be examining how different orders vary in their visitation to flowers of different types

## Intended Audience

This information is intended for people with access to a garden, whether is be a community garden or a small backyard/balcony one. I want to better inform the general public about what they could plant in order to best support pollinators.
